# Icons

The icons here come from [Lucide](https://lucide.dev), unmodified: its 24x24
stroke icons, drawn with `stroke="currentColor"`. They are compiled into the
app (`resources/resources.qrc`) and colored for the theme when they are loaded
(`src/app/Icons.cpp`).

- Source: `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/lucide-icons/lucide/main/icons/<name>.svg`
- Set: Lucide, commit `9cb3934c` on `main` (2026-11-26)
- Taken on: 2026-11-26
- License: ISC, and the MIT license for the icons that come from Feather. The
  full text is in `LICENSE.lucide`, and in the About box.

A few names are not what the thing they stand for would suggest:

- `trash.svg` is what `trash-2` used to be: Lucide has taken `trash-2` into
  `trash` as an alias.
- `eraser.svg` is "Delete for good", so that it can be told from "Delete".
- `square-pen.svg` is "Edit"; `notebook-pen` is too fine to read at 16 px.
- `chevrons-right.svg` and `chevrons-left.svg` are "Next tab" and "Previous tab".
